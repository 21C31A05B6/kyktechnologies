Our Process

How we place talent, end to end.

1

Understand

We start by learning the role, the team, and the outcomes that matter — not just a job description.

2

Source

We tap global networks and active outreach to reach candidates who aren't actively browsing job boards.

3

Evaluate

Structured screening for skills, working style, and long-term fit — not just keyword matching.

4

Match

We present a shortlist of candidates who genuinely fit the role and your organization's context.

5

Interview

We coordinate interviews and gather structured feedback from both sides to keep momentum.

6

Place

We support onboarding and check in after placement to make sure the fit is working.
