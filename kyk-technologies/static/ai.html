Long-term exploration

AGI — Artificial General Intelligence.

General-purpose intelligence — systems capable of matching and eventually exceeding human-level performance across a broad range of tasks — represents the next major frontier in technology. KYK is building knowledge, research capabilities and foresight around this evolving field.

General-Purpose Intelligence

Understanding how AI systems move from narrow task performance toward broad, transferable reasoning capabilities.

Reasoning Systems

Exploring architectures that enable multi-step reasoning, planning and decision-making across complex domains.

Autonomous Learning

Research into systems that can learn continuously from experience without constant human retraining or supervision.

Multi-Domain Intelligence

Tracking progress toward AI that generalizes knowledge across language, science, engineering and creative domains.

Human-Level Cognitive Capabilities

Studying what it means for machines to approach human-level understanding, judgment and adaptability.

Advanced AI Architectures

Following the research community's work on transformer successors, memory systems and hybrid neural-symbolic approaches.

Future research direction

ASI — Artificial Superintelligence.

Artificial Superintelligence — intelligence that would exceed human capability broadly across virtually all cognitive domains — remains a long-range frontier. KYK takes it seriously as a research and organizational preparation question: building the right knowledge today, well before such systems become plausible.

Advanced Machine Intelligence

Exploring the theoretical and architectural foundations of machine intelligence that surpasses human expert performance.

Autonomous Intelligent Systems

Understanding how systems with greater autonomy and self-direction will be designed, governed and deployed.

Superintelligent Architectures

Following early-stage research on the computational and architectural requirements for higher-order machine intelligence.

Advanced Reasoning

Researching how future systems might reason across scientific, mathematical and philosophical domains at superhuman levels.

AI Safety & Alignment

Studying how intelligent systems can be built to reliably pursue goals aligned with human values — a critical research area for the long-term.

Human-Machine Collaboration

Exploring interfaces, governance frameworks and social structures for a future where highly intelligent machines and humans work together.
